About this site

Learn Propulsion is a personal study site for AAE 33700, Aerospace Propulsion Foundations, at Purdue (Fall 2026). It was built from the course lecture slides, the equation sheet and the standards practice set. It is a study aid, not an official course page.

How the course is graded

Your grade comes from how many of the 17 standards you meet, not from points. Computational standards are graded Met / Minor revision needed (a 48-hour revision form) / New attempt required. J1 needs 8 of 10. Attempt at most 3 standards in a week, and do not wait until the end of the semester.

How the problem generators work

Each problem type draws random but realistic inputs, solves the problem exactly with the same relations as the equation sheet, and checks your answer within about 1% (2% for multi-step cycle results), so values read from the printed tables are accepted. After you answer you get the full worked solution and a fresh problem. Accuracy is saved only in your browser.

Conventions: air γ = 1.4, R = 287 J/kg·K, cp = 1004.5 J/kg·K unless stated; thermochemistry from Tables A-23, A-25 and A-27; convection correlations are the standard Incropera forms, plus the Bartz relation and the recovery factor from the equation sheet.
