Part 2Key relations and common mistakes

Key relations

  • \(\Delta v = g_0I_{sp}\ln MR\); \(m_p = m_{pl}\frac{MR-1}{MR - (MR-1)/\lambda}\), \(m_i = m_p(1-\lambda)/\lambda\).
  • \(\dot m = p_cA_t/c^*\), \(F = C_Fp_cA_t\), \(I_{sp} = C_Fc^*/g_0\); \(C_F = C_{F,vac} - (p_a/p_c)\varepsilon\); \(I_{sp} = I_{sp,vac} - p_aA_e/(\dot mg_0)\).
  • \(c^* = \sqrt{\bar RT_c/(\mathcal M\gamma)}\,(2/(\gamma+1))^{-(\gamma+1)/(2(\gamma-1))}\).
  • Solid: \(r_b = ap_c^n\), \(p_c = (a\rho_pA_bc^*/A_t)^{1/(1-n)}\).
  • Resistances \(L/kA\), \(\ln(r_2/r_1)/(2\pi kL)\), \(1/hA\); fins \(m^2 = hP/kA_c\), \(q_f = \sqrt{hPkA_c}\,\theta_b\tanh mL\), \(\eta_o = 1 - \frac{NA_f}{A_t}(1 - \eta_f)\).
  • \(T_{aw} = T_g(1 + r\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2)\), \(r = Pr^{1/3}\); internal flow \(\frac{T_s - T_{m,o}}{T_s - T_{m,i}} = e^{-\bar hPL/\dot mc_p}\).

Common mistakes

  • Using the air γ (1.4) for rocket exhaust; computing \(p_e/p_c\) on the subsonic branch.
  • Units of \(a\) in St. Robert's law (cm/s·MPa⁻ⁿ is not SI).
  • Choosing a correlation without computing Re first, or using \(T_g\) instead of \(T_{aw}\) for high-speed heat flux.
  • Fin perimeter and area mix-ups: \(A_c = \pi D^2/4\) and \(P = \pi D\) for a pin.
